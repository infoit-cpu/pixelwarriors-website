Questions

 

Is Arrow Out free?

Yes. The game is free. You can watch an optional video to get an extra heart, and a short ad may play between levels.

How do I play?

Tap an arrow to send it off the board in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, they bump and you lose a heart. Clear every arrow to win.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every board is made so that there is always an order that clears it.

Who are the other players in the league?

They are computer players, so the league works offline too.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
