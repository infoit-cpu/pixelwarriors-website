Three ways to play

Every match lasts two minutes and every city is built fresh, so no two games are the same.

CLASSIC

You and seven other holes. Get eaten and you come back smaller. The biggest hole when time runs out wins the crown.

ROYALE

No second chances: an eaten hole is out. Grow fast, stay away from bigger holes and be the last one standing.

SOLO

Just you and the city. Swallow as much of it as you can in two minutes and beat your best percentage.

Why you'll love it

Questions

 

Is Gobble Hole free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional video ads give you double coins, and a short ad may play between matches.

How do I play?

Put your finger anywhere on the screen and drag towards where you want to go. Things smaller than your hole fall in and make it bigger.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The other holes are computer players, so you can play without internet. Ads need a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
