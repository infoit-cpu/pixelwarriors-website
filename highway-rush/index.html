Questions

 

Is Highway Rush free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional video ads give you rewards such as a continue, free coins or double coins, and a short ad may play between races.

How do I play?

Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer (or use arrows or tilt, chosen in Settings). Hold NITRO to boost and BRAKE to slow down. Pass cars closely for near-miss points.

Does it work offline?

Yes, you can play without internet. Ads and ad rewards need a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
