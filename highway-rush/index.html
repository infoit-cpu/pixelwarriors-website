Questions

 

Is Highway Rush free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional video ads give you rewards such as free coins or double coins, and a short ad may play between races.

How do I play?

Hold GAS to drive and BRAKE to slow down or reverse, turn the steering wheel (or use arrows or tilt, chosen in Settings) and hold NITRO for a boost. Fall off the track and you are put back at the last checkpoint.

Does it work offline?

Yes, you can play without internet. Ads and ad rewards need a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
