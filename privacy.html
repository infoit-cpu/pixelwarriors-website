Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Pixel Warriors (Mithilesh Dubey, "we") makes free mobile games supported by ads. This policy applies to all our games, including Swarm Survivor, and explains what information is collected when you play.

Information our games store

Your progress (coins, upgrades, best times, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not have servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling a game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games show ads using Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • ad interactions

Google's use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
