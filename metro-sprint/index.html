Questions

 

Is Metro Sprint free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional video ads give you rewards such as a revive, free coins or double coins, and a short ad may play between runs.

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to roll. Double tap to ride your skateboard.

Does it work offline?

Yes, you can play without internet. Ads and ad rewards need a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
