Questions

 

Is Swarm Survivor free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional video ads give you rewards such as a revive, double coins or a free chest, and a short ad may play between levels.

Does it work offline?

Yes, you can play without internet. Ads and ad rewards need a connection.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 or newer with a 64-bit processor, which covers almost all phones sold in recent years.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
